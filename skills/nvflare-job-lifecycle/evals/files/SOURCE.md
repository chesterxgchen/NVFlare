These fixtures are synthetic, minimal exported-job and routing examples for
NVFLARE job lifecycle skill admission tests. They are intentionally small and
do not contain private data, generated benchmark outputs, or runnable training
datasets.

The exported-job folders model only enough structure for deterministic routing
and command-selection checks: `meta.json`, server/client app config files, and
small custom source files where needed to test framework-vs-lifecycle
boundaries.
